Setup Wizard · v1.0

Welcome

This wizard sets up the GlobalBLOCS quantitative research and trading-intelligence platform on this computer. It installs the local analytics engine, connects market-data sources, configures brokerage connections (paper trading by default), sets your risk limits, and secures the app with a passcode. You can change everything later in Settings.

You will need: Python 3.10 or newer, about 3 GB of free disk space, and an internet connection for the engine install. API keys (Polygon, FRED, E*TRADE, Plaid) are optional and can be added later.

License and risk disclosures

This software produces quantitative research, model forecasts and trading signals. They are not investment advice and do not guarantee any return. Trading securities involves risk of loss. Backtests and model results are hypothetical and may not reflect future results. Signals are labeled with their data source and whether data is real-time, delayed or simulated.
AI agents can only propose trades. Every live order requires your explicit approval with a typed confirmation phrase, passes automated risk checks, and is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log. Brokerage passwords are never seen or stored by this app.

Brokerage connections

Paper trading is always on. Live connections use OAuth through official or authorized APIs and start read-only.

E*TRADE — official API (OAuth 1.0a). Request a consumer key from E*TRADE's developer portal; use the sandbox until your production key is approved.
Chase / J.P. Morgan Self-Directed Investing — Chase does not offer a public trading API, so this connection is read-only (balances, holdings, transactions, dividends) through an aggregator you authorize.

Risk limits

Orders that break any limit are rejected before they reach a broker. Live trading stays disabled until you enable it in Settings.

Security

Secrets are encrypted with your operating system's keychain. Set a passcode to sign in to the dashboard and approve orders.
